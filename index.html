KNOW BEFORE
YOU GO.

The best trip starts with realistic expectations, thoughtful preparation and a responsible local operator.

↗

WHEN TO TRAVEL

Follow the seasons

Wildlife movement changes throughout the year. Choose dates based on the experience you hope for, and confirm current local conditions.

⌁

WHAT TO PACK

Keep it practical

Bring layered neutral clothing, sun protection, binoculars, comfortable shoes and any personal medication you need.

✳

TRAVEL RESPONSIBLY

Leave no trace

Follow your guide's instructions, keep a safe distance, never feed wildlife and respect park rules and local communities.

For current visitor rules, fees and park updates, consult Tanzania National Parks (TANAPA).

GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU
TAKE OFF.

A few useful starting points. Confirm the details for your dates with your operator before travelling.

When is the best time to visit?

It depends on your priorities. The migration is seasonal and its exact location varies; the Serengeti offers wildlife viewing year-round. Ask a local operator about your travel dates.

Can I guarantee seeing the Big Five?

No. Wildlife is free-ranging and sightings can never be guaranteed. A skilled guide can help you understand animal behaviour and improve your chances responsibly.

Can I book a tour through this website?

Not yet. This is a front-end demonstration. The enquiry form opens your email app; live bookings require a real operator, confirmed prices and a secure booking workflow.

What should I know about safety?

Follow park rules and your guide's instructions, remain in the vehicle where required, never approach or feed wildlife, and check official travel and health guidance before departure.
